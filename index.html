Who we are

A light in the darkness.

Harmony Life International is a registered 501(c)3 charity working in Uganda Africa. We equip people in need with educational and entrepreneurial opportunities, make basic medical care accessible, and sharing hope with the oppressed.

Read our full story

Our Ministries

Several ministries. One calling: to love people well.

Child Sponsorship

Harmony Life Kids!

Nurturing care, school fees and a family for vulnerable youth.

Harmony Life Homes

Safe, permanent shelter for families in crisis.

Manna Care

Community-built medical missions, food assistance and crisis relief.

EMPOWER

Equipping, mentoring and providing opportunities to women in entrepreneurship.

Worship & Arts

Discipling young artists through song and community.
