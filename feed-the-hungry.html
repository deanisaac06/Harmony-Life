Feed the Hungry

Elderly women and single-mom or child-led households can be especially vulnerable to food insecurity. We make sure they are seen, cherished and valued.

The need

Nobody else to turn to.

Our Feed the Hungry program provides food and home visits to those who have nobody else who hears their cries. Beyond hungry tummies, we offer a listening ear, prayers and somebody to reach out to. Each Christmas alone, our relief deliveries reach more than 35 households.
