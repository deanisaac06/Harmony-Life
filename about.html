Who We Are

About Us

The story of how a family history in Uganda became a calling — and a calling became Harmony Life International.

Who

A registered charity

Harmony Life International is a registered 501(c)3 non-profit charitable organization.

What

Hope, healing, transformation

We work alongside those in need, equipping and empowering them with educational and entrepreneurial opportunities, and strive to make basic medical care accessible to as many people as possible.

Where

Fort Portal & Kampala

Our work is focused around Fort Portal and Kampala, Uganda, East Africa — with outreach and fundraising events in the US.

When

Since 2012

Started in 2012 by Anne and Jeff Dean as the Harmony Life Foundation; expanded in 2015 into the public charity, Harmony Life International.

Why

A calling from family history

Co-founder Anne Dean’s family history in Uganda dates back before the regime of Idi Amin. Years of witnessing the country’s challenges called her to meet them head on.

Motto

“Promote Harmony, Speak Life!”

Everything we do — from a sewing class to a medical camp — is in service of this one sentence.

Our love for Uganda

We work directly with Harmony Life Uganda, a registered Ugandan NGO led by our team on the ground.
