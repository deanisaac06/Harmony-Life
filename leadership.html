Who We Are

Leadership

An amazing team of hard-working, dedicated men and women — all with huge hearts and a desire to transform lives.

Harmony Life International was founded by Anne Dean in 2012. In 2013 Harmony Life Uganda was registered in the country of Uganda.

Kenneth Bagonza manages general operations on the ground in Uganda.

Anne Dean manages general operations in the US.

Executive Director, Harmony Life Intl., USA

Anne Dean

Anne manages Harmony Life’s operations in the US, carrying a family history in Uganda that stretches back generations into a lifelong calling.

Executive Director, Harmony Life Uganda

Kenneth Bagonza

Kenneth leads Harmony Life’s day-to-day work on the ground in Uganda.

Board & Volunteers

We also have amazing officers on our board and many wonderful volunteers who work hands-on — ministering, fundraising and praying for all of Harmony Life’s work.
