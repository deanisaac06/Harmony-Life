Missions and Outreach

Do you love experiencing different cultures, learning new things, sharing your talents and serving God and others?

Support a TripAsk about joining us

Adventure Mission Trips

A journey of thousands of miles starts with the first step.

If you love experiencing different cultures, learning new things and sharing your talents in service, our Adventure Mission trips might be right for you. If you think you might be interested in traveling with us, let us know — we would love to have you join us.

We also run Medical Mission trips more specifically focused toward those in the medical field — see our Manna Care page for details.

20 years in the making

Sponsor Steve Jenkins began supporting Ken Bagonza as a child — and 20 years later, after 15 years apart, Steve finally traveled to Uganda to meet him face to face.

Outreach Events

Music, dance, preaching & healing.

One of the most powerful ways we impact communities is through outreach events — drawing crowds of hundreds through music, dance, preaching and healing ministries. Every event is unique, humbling, and lives are transformed. If you’re interested in participating — preaching, singing or sponsoring — let us know. Just be ready to work hard, and be prepared to be blessed beyond measure.

Street Ministry

Meeting people exactly where they are.

Some of our most sacred work is going out to meet those living on the streets. We pack healthy lunches, snacks and bits of encouragement, and meet on street corners to talk, pray and get to know each other a little better. Life on the streets of Uganda is not easy — so many men, women and children are really suffering. Our prayer is to keep following God’s lead in how we can be his hands and feet.

A day to rest, too

As with our Adventure Missions, we build in a day of rest to see a bit of the country — the wildlife, the falls, the culture.
