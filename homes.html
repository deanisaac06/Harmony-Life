Harmony Life Homes

Illness, desperation and disease are products of poverty. These homes save lives.

Before & after

A witness of God’s love.

An elderly woman lived with her great-grandson in a home that was literally falling down around them, with no funds and no way to repair it. Their new home is what safe shelter makes possible.

Approximate cost to build one home

How we build

The FinnBuild method.

After extensive research into building processes, we chose the FinnBuild method for most of our homes and projects — a build-as-you-go process that requires no bricks made in advance. The FinnBuilder form is simple, highly portable, and works with little waste and very little finishing work. It uses more cement, but the final product is strong, high quality, and not much costlier than traditional local brick.

This process won the All Africa Innovative Housing Competition, run by the South African Government, the NHBRC and ABSA Bank — and we’re the first to bring it to the Fort Portal area.

A story of resilience

Monica’s Blessing

This family of five lived in one tiny, dilapidated room. After losing her job, Monica — a single mom — struggled to care for her children. Now, they have secure housing and all of the kids are attending school thanks to loving sponsors.

Another story of resilience

Beth’s Home

Beth is a single mother who raised her children in a home that couldn’t keep out the rain. Through Harmony Life Homes, she received a safe, permanent house of her own.

Furnishing a home

In addition to the home cost, a bed with a mattress can make a real difference for a family’s health and rest. When extra support is available, we are able to add these essential comforts alongside a safe home.

Why we build

Because every family deserves a place to feel safe.

Over the years, the need for safe, secure shelter has come to our attention time and time again. We’ve seen families living in conditions that are difficult to imagine and almost impossible to survive without help.

  • A single mom trying to survive in one small 6’ x 8’ room with 4 children
  • A seriously ill child having no hygienic place to lay their head
  • A very young boy living on the roadside with his dying father

We have been able to come alongside many families to provide home – permanent structures where they can feel safe and live without fear.

When people have secure housing they can focus on the other demands of life knowing that they have a place to call home.
