Harmony Life Homes

Illness, desperation and disease are products of poverty. These homes save lives.

How we build

The FinnBuild method.

After extensive research into building processes, we chose the FinnBuild method — a build-as-you-go process that requires no bricks made in advance. The FinnBuilder form is simple, highly portable, and works with little waste and very little finishing work. It uses more cement, but the final product is strong, high quality, and not much costlier than traditional local brick.

This process won the All Africa Innovative Housing Competition, run by the South African Government, the NHBRC and ABSA Bank — and we’re the first to bring it to the Fort Portal area.

A story of resilience

Monica’s Blessing

This family of five lived in one tiny, dilapidated room. After losing her job, Monica — a single mom — struggled to care for her children. Now, they have secure housing and all of the kids are attending school thanks to loving sponsors. Monica is learning a trade through our EMPOWER program, too.

Another story of resilience

Beth’s Home

Beth is a single mother who raised her children in a home that couldn’t keep out the rain. Through Harmony Life Homes, she received a safe, permanent house of her own.
